CWD Index

  • Bird Sounds

    Immediately standing out to me, Kyle McDonald and Manny Tan's Bird Sounds from 2017 is a website that uses a computer to automatically sort thousands of bird sounds without even being given the names. It places similar bird sounds close together, often creating a symphony of chirps.

  • Iceberger

    This is another fun standout. Iceberger, by Joshua Tauberer, is a 2D physics simulator that lets you draw an iceberg and then see how it floats.
